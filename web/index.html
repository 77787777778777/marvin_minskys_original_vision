how to play

Every room, object, character, verb and even the unfolding ritual in this game is a Minsky frame (MIT AI Memo 306, A Framework for Representing Knowledge, 1974). The engine runs unmodified Tcl — the same adventure.tcl you can play in a terminal — inside the Feather WASM interpreter.

The aim: six deeds and six insights, for a score of 12. Deeds come from solving the puzzles the house sets you; insights come from talking to its people about what they know (each genuinely new fact you learn earns one).

Stuck? Type hint (or clues) for a nudge toward what people could still tell you. Almost every noun in the game is also a topic you can ask the curator about — he answers from anywhere, not just his library. If a topic misses, he'll list what he does know.

Educational verbs: why shows which frames and defaults the engine is running on right now; frame (alone) lists Minsky's vocabulary and frame <term> explains one term and points at where the house embodies it; view re-frames a room through a perspective you've been taught. help lists every verb.
